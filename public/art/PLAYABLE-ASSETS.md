## Visual and geometry checks

The actual original city WebPs were inspected before generation. Final encoded WebPs were then inspected at full size.

- Hong Kong: the foreground bicycle, restaurant tables and mint stools, striped awning, signs, balconies, central lamp, stone steps, skyline, shoreline, and boats remain in the same locations. The sun is gone; blue night sky and cooler harbor reflections replace sunset lighting. Amber restaurant light remains warm.
- Shenzhen: the restaurant tables, terraces and umbrellas, sign, avenue, paired walkers, skyline, transit entrance/escalator, fountain, and curved planter edges remain in the same locations. The orange sunset band is gone; blue night sky and amber local lighting preserve a similarly inviting atmosphere.
- Geometry spot check: seven 80 × 80 pixel edge patches in each city were compared with the original over ±12 pixels of displacement using normalized image-gradient correlation. Every tested patch had its best match at (0, 0). Hong Kong patch correlations ranged 0.659–0.945; Shenzhen ranged 0.738–0.904. Tested anchors included restaurant sign/table, street or avenue, transit/fountain, awning, and boat. This is an anchor check, not a proof that every pixel is geometrically identical.
- Small painterly details, light reflections, cloud shape, flowers, and people can vary slightly after a generative lighting edit. A discrete time-of-day replacement is appropriate; do not promise pixel-perfect morphing or crossfade alignment. Keep original imagery available as the fallback.
- Jun's final alpha ranges from 0 to 255. All four corner pixels are fully transparent; 221,265 of 614,400 pixels are fully transparent. Cream and dark-teal compositing checks show a clean cutout, including hair and glasses, without a background rectangle. Warm rim lighting is intentional. Both hands and the complete blank ochre sketchbook are visible. The character has one mild, friendly listening expression, without evaluative reaction poses.
