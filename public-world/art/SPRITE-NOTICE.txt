Crossing Lives — original character artwork

player.webp and friend.webp reuse the original, previously approved Crossing Lives character assets. These are small in-world characters, not portraits of real people. The original artwork was created with OpenAI image generation. No external character, logo or game art is used.

The family cast uses a simple generic pixel silhouette for its fictional child; it does not depict a real child. Cast choices make no claim about fares, eligibility, preferences, mobility or income.

Scene illustrations are separately reviewed original assets. A labelled functional schematic is shown when a scene illustration has not yet been approved or is unavailable.
