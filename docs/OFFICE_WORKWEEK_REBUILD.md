# Bounded office workweek

The Office notebook now compares two home bases for one fictional adult with the same Hong Kong job and pay. Native radios choose 2 or 4 office days per week; an adjacent labelled native select shows time or money over exactly four weeks. The selected scene party does not enter the calculation. The compact primary view puts the controls and two result quantities together. Only this office component hides the shell's duplicate room kicker. Calendar and route geometry are available through an optional disclosure, with evidence below.

| Office days/week | Return days | Hong Kong home | Shenzhen home |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 8 | 12 hours; 320 HKD | 28 hours; 800 HKD |
| 4 | 16 | 24 hours; 640 HKD | 56 hours; 1,600 HKD |

Invented daily return allowances are 90 minutes/40 HKD for Hong Kong and 210 minutes/100 HKD for Shenzhen. No number is an actual fare, measured route, rent, eligibility finding or building-occupancy claim. The notebook makes no best-home recommendation and changes neither the outing clock nor outing money.

The calendar contains four rows of seven equal cells and marks 8 or 16 office return days. Route bars start at the same origin and retain a fixed maximum within each measure: 56 hours or 1,600 HKD. Changing office days doubles each rendered bar; changing the selected measure changes their ratio. Two headline values always share one unit; time and money are never summed.

## State and scope

The hook-free interaction wrapper adds only the office branch. The office component uses the existing values/onChange contract with officeWorkweekDays and officeWorkweekMeasure. It preserves unrelated keys, defaults invalid state to two days/time, and writes no storage. It has no dependency on the selected cast, day, scenario, other decisions or legacy commute modules. The old generic office lens remains as a preserved authored fixture, not the live Office notebook. Other eleven live routes and their models are unchanged by this delta.
