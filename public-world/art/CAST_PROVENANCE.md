# Original fictional cast

The three PNG cutouts in `cast-manifest.json` were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generator on 7 October 2026. Original bytes, transparency and pixel dimensions are retained. The manifest records SHA-256 hashes and measured visible-body and shoe-contact bounds. Existing `player.webp`, `friend.webp` and `neighborhood-lane.webp` supplied the style references.

- `older-adult-a.png`: fictional older adult in an olive jacket
- `older-adult-b.png`: fictional older adult in a plum jacket
- `child.png`: fictional child in an ochre hoodie, drawn with child proportions

These are single idle poses facing right, not sprite sheets or walk cycles. The game mirrors around the same ground anchor and uses its existing restrained movement bob. Reduced-motion settings disable that bob.

Older couple uses the two older-adult images. Family uses the original adults plus the child, at the existing 0.67 adult-height ratio. Other choices retain the original adult characters. Appearances do not change speed, costs, fares, eligibility, preferences, needs or calculations. Child fares and eligibility remain unknown.

Original images and document-sized edge samples were visually reviewed before integration. Source checks do not replace actual game review at 360, 390 and 1440 pixels, including both directions and light/dark scene backgrounds.
