These lossless WebP conversions were verified against the approved generated PNG sources for exact decoded RGBA equality. They preserve the original pixels and alpha, including a faint colored sprite edge that requires review at the final rendered size. No manual edge treatment was applied.

The 2 × 2 sprite sheet has 627 × 627 cells. Runtime uses only top-left idle-right and bottom-right idle-back. Their measured foot anchors are (330, 600) and (290.5, 583) within their cells. The two similar stride frames are not represented as a finished walk cycle. Node translation uses static idle poses and becomes immediate for reduced motion.

The Shenzhen images share geometry and provide illustrative day/evening lighting. The authored 18:00 arrival threshold is not an astronomical sunset claim. Buildings, restaurants and streets are imagined; the current journey is modeled rail via Lo Wu to an illustrative Luohu dinner, never an implicit Futian route. The Hong Kong illustration is reused with destination framing for an ordinary nearby local dinner. Existing painted people are background NPCs; the small ochre/navy person is the controllable player.

Artwork, physical objects and player foot anchors share one source-image transform. Interface labels, node buttons, clocks and route diagrams remain semantic HTML. Unknown entry eligibility and service availability stay unknown.
